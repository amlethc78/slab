Glossary

Plain-English explanations of every metric.

The basics

Raw priceWhat an ungraded copy sells for on eBay right now.
PSA 10 marginDollar difference between the PSA 10 price and the raw price.
Gem rate% of submitted copies that receive PSA 10. Higher = grades more easily.

The S.L.A.B. score

S.L.A.B. scoreComposite score combining gem rate, margin, pop scarcity, PSA 9 floor safety, demand, and stability. S = best, C = weakest.

PSA 9 floor — your downside protection

PSA 9 floor gapHow far the PSA 9 price is above/below your break-even. Positive = costs covered even if you miss the 10.
✓ SafePSA 9 covers your full cost.
~ CloseWithin 15% of break-even.
✗ RiskPSA 9 does not cover costs.
